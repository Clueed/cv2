# STRABAG

**What it is**: STRABAG SE — a Vienna-headquartered, publicly listed European construction and infrastructure services group. One of the largest construction companies in Europe, spanning building construction, civil engineering, road construction, underground/tunnelling, bridge building, construction-material production, project development, and building management.

**Scale** (per the LinkedIn clipping, 2026-07-03):
- ~89,000 people worldwide / "rund 86.000" in the German copy, across 2,400+ locations. LinkedIn shows 22,305 total employees on the platform (a registered-subset figure, not the true headcount) and 14,555 profiles.
- 369,408 LinkedIn followers.
- Company-wide 2-year headcount growth ~13%; Business Development sub-function ~8%. Median employee tenure 5.1 years.
- Hires notably from **Technische Universität Berlin** (13 hires cited) — same institution where [[entities/mark-vavulov|Mark]] is enrolled in a paused CS B.Sc. ^[inferred relevance]

**Financial moment**: Best financial results to date in 2025, driven by infrastructure construction (mobility, energy, water) and high-tech industrial construction. Headwinds cited: delayed German budget approvals, lack of Austrian municipal funding. ^[extracted from clipping]

**Strategic posture**:
- **Decarbonization**: science-based emissions targets; public goal of **climate-neutral (klimaneutral) by 2040**. The construction industry is framed in STRABAG's own copy as causing ~38% of global CO₂ emissions, positioning decarbonization tech as a core corporate-development lever.
- **Innovation self-image**: "most innovative and sustainable construction technology company in Europe" — the "construction technology" framing matters for how the corporate-development / scouting function is scoped (technology trends, innovative technologies/products, market developments, and M&A targets all sit in one team). ^[inferred]

**Competitive set** (per clipping): Vinci SA, ACS Group, Skanska AB — competing on technological innovation and financial strength.

## Corporate Development — "Scouting & Cooperations"

The hiring team is **Scouting & Cooperations**, part of STRABAG **Unternehmensentwicklung** (Corporate Development). Its mandate, per the JD: identify and evaluate technology trends, innovative technologies/products, market developments, company targets, and stake/cooperation opportunities to shape the group's long-term success. The specific sub-team hiring is **Target Scouting**, feeding **deal sourcing for M&A processes**. See [[references/strabag-target-scouting-analyst-vienna-jd]] for the full role analysis.
