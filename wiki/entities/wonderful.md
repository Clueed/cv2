# Wonderful

**What it is**: Agentic-AI platform that lets enterprises **build, test, deploy, and monitor AI agents** for complex needs, serving customers and employees across **voice, chat, email, and back-office systems** in any language or cultural context ^[extracted]. Positions itself at the shift "from Generative AI to Agentic AI" — moving enterprises from GenAI experiments to deployed agentic outcomes.

**Go-to-market**: Enterprise, top-down. Sells into large enterprises via CxO / IT-leader / C-level relationships, complex multi-stakeholder buying cycles, procurement-and-legal-heavy deals, and partner-led motions with **System Integrators and Hyperscalers**. Targets **€500k+ deals**. Building out country-level founding GTM teams (Austria seat tracked in [[references/wonderful-gtm-austria-jd]]).

**Stage / scale** ^[inferred]: Early-stage, expansion-mode startup — "local founding team," "early-stage share options," "limited oversight," and country-by-country territory build all point to an early commercial phase. Self-describes (or is "tipped") as "the fastest growing startup in Europe"; this is a marketing claim, unverified, with no funding / ARR / headcount disclosed in the source JD. The team is described as "high-agency builders coming from the best tech companies."

**Stack signal**: Uses **Salesforce** for account data / forecasting (named in the JD). English + German used at executive level for the DACH/Austria market.
