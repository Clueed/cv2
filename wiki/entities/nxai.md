# NXAI

**Type**: European AI research-to-industry company (foundation models / sovereign AI)
**HQ**: Linz, Oberösterreich, Austria
**Core tech**: Next-generation **xLSTM** architectures
**Flagship model**: **TiRex** — zero-shot time-series foundation model, claimed up to **50x more efficient** than traditional transformer models
**Positioning**: Sovereign, **on-premise** alternative to US hyperscalers for industrial enterprises
**Target ICP**: Industry 4.0, Robotics, Energy
**Stage signal**: Building its commercial engine now ("as we scale and build our commercial engine") — early commercial team, founding GTM roles open

## What's verified (from the JD)

- Based in Linz, Austria; positions itself as "a leading center for European AI innovation, turning advanced research into real-world industrial applications."
- Core architecture bet is **xLSTM** (extended LSTM), explicitly framed against the transformer paradigm.
- **TiRex** is a **zero-shot time-series foundation model** for industrial enterprises, marketed on efficiency (up to 50x vs transformers).
- Go-to-market thesis is **sovereign / on-premise European AI** as an alternative to US hyperscalers — a data-sovereignty and efficiency play into regulated/industrial buyers.
- Commercial org is early: a **CRO** plus AEs, and this RevOps/BizDev seat is framed as the "operational backbone of the Sales Team." Founding-team, ground-up-processes language throughout.
- Comp anchor: base **from EUR 4,643** (Austrian monthly convention → roughly **€65K/yr gross at 14 salaries** ^[inferred]) plus a variable OTE component and a **VSOP** (virtual stock option plan). Klimaticket or parking covered; relocation + visa support offered.
