# All But Sugar GmbH

**What**: Early-stage food-tech building the "first modular platform and process technology for sugar alternatives that replace sucrose 1:1" — no sugar, no synthetic sweeteners, high in fiber, reduced calories, and EFSA-compliant.
**Stated goal**: First European, then global market leader in **full replacement sweetening systems**.
**Location**: Vienna, Austria — central location at Hauptbahnhof.
**Size**: 1-10 employees (per the join.com sidebar).
**Sector**: Food & beverage manufacturing (Nahrungsmittel- und Getränkeherstellung).
**Recruiting contact**: Lisa Reiss (Recruiting Team).

## What is known

- **Positioning ("two fronts")** — the company frames itself as winning on two fronts at once: "technology so far ahead that it sells itself, and a sales engine built to turn that edge into revenue at scale." So it is simultaneously a deep-tech/process-tech play *and* a commercial-scaling play. ^[inferred: the "sales engine" emphasis implies commercial build is a live, near-term priority, not future]
- **Product shape** — a *modular platform + process technology*, i.e. a B2B sweetening-system supplier selling into food brands and via distributors, not (primarily) a consumer CPG brand. The JD's "identify new sales opportunities from distributors to brands" and "pricing conversation with a co-manufacturer" confirm a B2B / ingredient-supplier commercial model. ^[inferred]
- **Regulatory anchor** — EFSA-compliant is stated as a product property; the EU food-additive / novel-food regulatory surface is therefore load-bearing to the business.
- **Stage** — 1-10 FTE, "a company still being built," "ownership from day one," investor updates + fundraising support + grant applications all in scope → **early-stage, pre/early-revenue, actively fundraising and grant-seeking**. ^[inferred]
- **Tooling** — the company runs on Google Drive, ClickUp, Pipedrive, Canva, and **Claude** (named explicitly in the JD).
