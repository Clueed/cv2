# Lago

**What it is**: Lago is an open-source billing platform. It positions itself as the leading OSS billing/metering infrastructure for tech companies, letting businesses build flexible (notably usage-based / metered) pricing models and scale their monetization. Developer-tools / fintech-infrastructure shape: the product is sold to and adopted by engineering-heavy teams, and the company runs a large open-source community as its primary distribution engine.

**Scale & backing** (per the careers clipping, 2026-07-08):
- **$22M+ raised** from top-tier investors including **Y Combinator** and **FirstMark**.
- **~10K GitHub stars** — an "active and growing developer community" is the stated distribution moat.
- Named customers: **PayPal, CoreWeave, Synthesia, Mistral.ai** (and, in the "Why Join" section, **Groq**). AI-infra and AI-product companies feature heavily in the logo set.
- **"Barely spent any money on Marketing or Ads so far"** — self-described. Growth to date has been product-/community-/founder-led, which is the context for hiring a Growth Chief of Staff to build the paid/outbound/brand motions from scratch. ^[inferred]

**How they work / culture** (from the JD):
- Fully **remote**, with the Growth CoS role scoped to **Remote Europe** (applicants must be based in Europe). "Frequent trips to the US."
- **12+ nationalities** on the team; explicitly building a "global company with a global culture."
- Stated values: reliability, proactivity, ownership, "slope over intercept," accountability, "try fast / fail fast but learn fast." AI-first framing throughout ("Use AI tools and automation, while keeping humans in the loop").
- Competitive salary **plus stock options**.

**Strategic moment**: Framing the company around the AI-driven shift in monetization ("Monetization systems are shifting with AI"). Usage-based / metered billing is structurally aligned with how AI products (token / inference / usage metering) charge, which is why the AI-infra logo set is dense. ^[inferred]
