## Job Description (verbatim)

> **About Biolyz**
>
> Biolyz is a full-stack saliva-based biomarker testing company on a mission to accelerate access to biomarker analytics for all humans, globally. We offer a non-invasive testing solution whereby athletes and coaches simply use a cotton swab to collect saliva, which we analyze via mass spectrometry within 48 hours across 60+ biomarkers covering stress, recovery, metabolism, and inflammation. Our platform delivers real-time, hyper-personalized insights directly to coaches and athletes. Having tested over 2.5 million biomarkers across teams in the Champions League, Bundesliga, Premier League and Ligue 1 and other sports, Biolyz has powered the best athletes in their field.
>
> **About the Role**
>
> We're looking for a Founder's Associate to work directly alongside our CEO and help drive the most critical initiatives across the business. This isn't a traditional chief-of-staff role — it's a high-impact, high-ownership position for someone who thrives in ambiguity, moves fast, and gets things done. You'll be in the room (and on the calls) where it matters most: strategy sessions, key partnerships, investor conversations, and operational problem-solving.
>
> You'll act as a force multiplier for the CEO — tracking priorities, connecting dots across teams, and making sure nothing falls through the cracks. This role is ideal for someone who has seen how high-performance organizations operate — whether in investment banking, consulting, or a fast-moving startup — and is now ready to build something meaningful in the intersection of sports science, biotech, and data.
>
> Expect a steep learning curve, long hours, and the kind of exposure that would take years to get anywhere else.
>
> **What You'll Do**
>
> - Work side-by-side with the CEO on the company's most important strategic and operational priorities
> - Own and track critical action items across all functions, ensuring nothing is dropped or delayed
> - Prepare materials for board meetings, investor updates, key client presentations, and internal reviews
> - Conduct research and build business cases to support strategic decisions (new markets, partnerships, product direction)
> - Identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies across the business and drive solutions proactively
> - Represent the CEO in internal meetings and with external stakeholders when needed
> - Act as a trusted thought partner — challenging assumptions, stress-testing plans, and asking the right questions
>
> **What We're Looking For**
>
> - 2–3 years of experience in investment banking, management consulting, or a high-growth startup
> - Exceptional business acumen — you understand strategy, finance, and operations, and you connect them naturally
> - An extreme sense of urgency — you treat every day like it counts, because it does
> - Highly organized and structured, with the ability to manage multiple complex workstreams simultaneously
> - Strong written and verbal communication skills — crisp, clear, and confident with executives and external partners
> - Comfortable with long hours and high pressure — this role is not a 9-to-5, and you wouldn't have it any other way
> - Proactive and self-directed — you don't wait to be told what to do; you see what needs to happen and make it happen
>
> **Nice to Have**
>
> - Experience in sports, sports science, elite athletics, or working with sports organizations
> - Background in laboratory operations, biotech, life sciences, or diagnostics
> - Familiarity with data, analytics, or BI tools (e.g. SQL, Looker, Tableau, or similar)
> - Prior exposure to B2B SaaS, healthtech, or fast-scaling hardware + software businesses
>
> **Why Biolyz**
>
> - Direct access to the CEO from day one — real ownership, not busy work
> - A front-row seat to building a category-defining company in biomarker analytics
> - Tight-knit team with deep expertise in sports science, lab tech, and data
> - The chance to work with elite sports teams and world-class athletes
> - Fast progression for the right person — this role is a launchpad, not a landing spot

## What this role actually is

Weighted reading of the JD:

| Pillar | Weight | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic research & business cases | ~25% | Research + build business cases for new markets, partnerships, product direction. An **analytical/strategy** pillar heavier than ENPULSION's; the closest thing to a "real work product," not just coordination. |
| Priority tracking & execution follow-through | ~25% | Own and track critical action items across all functions; "nothing falls through the cracks." Classic PMO / chief-of-staff mechanic. |
| Exec / investor / client materials | ~20% | Board meetings, investor updates, key client presentations, internal reviews. Deck-craft, but framed as decision materials rather than the twice-named "advanced PowerPoint" of ENPULSION. |
| Bottleneck-hunting & operational problem-solving | ~15% | Identify inefficiencies, drive solutions proactively. The productization-of-bespoke-work pillar ([[user_productization_affinity]]). |
| CEO proxy & external representation | ~10% | Represent the CEO internally and with external stakeholders. Scope/authority unspecified. |
| Thought partner / stress-testing | ~5% (pervasive) | Challenge assumptions, stress-test plans. The "honest, structured input" mode, same as ENPULSION's Execution-Partner framing. |

Effective role: **CEO-orbit Founder's Associate with a genuine strategy/analysis pillar**, no direct reports, no commercial (sales/marketing/quota) pillar. The JD explicitly disavows the "traditional chief-of-staff" label and leans on ownership, ambiguity-tolerance, and business-case work. This is a **greener "GM-in-training" shape** than ENPULSION: business cases + thought-partner + external representation outweigh pure slide-polishing, which was ENPULSION's yellow flag.

## Application angle if pursued

Differentiator vs typical Founder's-Associate applicants (ex-IB / ex-consulting generalists):

1. **Founding operator > analyst** — Mark has lived inside a founding team for ~2.5 years and contributed to strategy, ops, customer, *and* fundraising work simultaneously ($20K → $1.5M ARR, 1 → 35 customers, 1 → 10 FTEs). IB/consulting applicants bring frameworks and deck reps; Mark brings reps from a real operating environment, including inside board and investor conversations. That is "seen how high-performance organizations operate — in a fast-moving startup" as lived experience, not observation.
2. **Business acumen is genuinely tri-lateral** — lead with the one line the JD asks for verbatim: strategy + finance + operations connected naturally. Anchor finance on the CBS Financial Management degree and the quantitative IFRS thesis; anchor ops on the RevOps cadence and capacity/forecast modeling; anchor strategy on building a category and GTM function from zero.
3. **Business-case machinery already built** — the "research + build business cases for new markets/partnerships/product direction" pillar is exactly the Europe-wide ICP scoring engine ([[skills/ai-powered-sales-tooling]]) plus the partner-channel build. Reframe the ICP engine as a **market/opportunity-screening system**, not a sales tool — same machine, pointed at "which market/partner next" instead of "which account next."
4. **Board/investor materials are a done rep** — fundraising-support work (decks, data room, DD Q&A, board reporting) maps near-verbatim to "prepare materials for board meetings and investor updates." Hold the [[feedback_finance_claims_scope|finance-claims discipline]] (founder-side deal mechanics yes; no LP-side / no fund model from scratch).
5. **Why-Biolyz narrative via the fitness bridge** — this is the differentiator ENPULSION couldn't offer. Mark's genuine fitness/veganism interest is an authentic hook into athlete performance / recovery / metabolism. Land it as a real "I care about this domain" story, *not* a claim of sports-science or lab expertise (per [[feedback_no_self_hedging]] — state the interest directly; don't hedge it, and don't fabricate domain reps).

**Counter-signals to weigh before applying:**
- The double domain gap (sports *and* wet-lab/diagnostics) is wider than any single-domain gap tracked. The fitness bridge covers half; the regulated-health-data / lab-ops half has zero reps and no personal-interest cover.
- No commercial pillar means the engineer-seller thesis stops compounding inside this seat. The bet is purely on the Founder's-Associate-as-launchpad progression being the green-flag shape.
- Location and comp are unknown and could be hard blockers (non-European base, on-site requirement outside Berlin, low cash).
- The "long hours, not a 9-to-5" framing is explicit and repeated — a real lifestyle question, not filler.
