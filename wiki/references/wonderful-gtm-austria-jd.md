# Wonderful — GTM (Enterprise Closer, Austria / Hybrid)

**Title posted**: GTM (f/m/x)
**Team**: S&M; local founding team for the Austria market
**Location**: Austria; hybrid (home office + travel to customers in Austria); listed location type "Remote"
**Type**: Full-time
**Comp**: "Great salary, excellent commission structure, and early-stage share options" (no band published)
**Source**: Wonderful careers (Ashby), captured 2026-06-21 from user paste
**JD URL**: [jobs.ashbyhq.com/wonderful/d3fcedcf…](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/wonderful/d3fcedcf-2922-4a54-b6ea-efec26430104)

This page captures the JD verbatim and analyses the seat against [[synthesis/mark-progression-over-direction]] and [[synthesis/mark-job-direction-map]]. It is the **highest-ACV pure-hunter / closer JD tracked** in the wiki: a founding-team enterprise sales seat at an agentic-AI startup, gated on a record of closing large, complex, multi-stakeholder enterprise deals and aiming at €500k+ first logos. It is the closest sibling to [[references/fonio-gtm-engineer-jd|fonio's GTM Engineer]] in *company shape* (early-stage AI, DACH, founding-adjacent) but its *opposite in mode*: fonio builds the selling machine, Wonderful is the person at the table closing the deal.

## What this role actually is

Weighted reading of the JD:

| Pillar | Weight | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| Net-new enterprise closing (hunting) | ~45% | Open net-new relationships and land first logos across Austria; aim at €500k+ deals. This is the load-bearing pillar — the seat exists to put logos and ARR on the board from a standing start. |
| Complex deal navigation (procurement / legal / pricing) | ~20% | Lead commercial discussions across pricing models, legal terms, and procurement in enterprise environments. Multi-stakeholder buying-cycle management end-to-end. |
| Executive relationship-building (CxO / C-level) | ~15% | Build trusted relationships with CxOs, IT leaders, and C-suite. Credibility-through-outcomes selling to senior stakeholders. |
| Account expansion / land-and-expand | ~10% | Own and grow strategic accounts into "long-term success stories"; drive measurable growth within owned accounts. |
| Partner-led motion (SIs / Hyperscalers) | ~5% | Joint enterprise deals with System Integrators and Hyperscalers — an explicit *required* qualification, so heavier than its time-weight suggests. |
| CRM hygiene / forecasting | ~5% | Maintain accurate account data, forecasts, use cases, timelines in Salesforce. |

Effective role: a **founding enterprise Account Executive for the Austria market** — a pure hunter/closer carrying a number, not a builder, strategist, or manager. No headcount to manage, no systems to build (handed a "cutting-edge agentic solution"), no analytical remit. Leverage comes entirely through closing complex deals and the relationships that produce them. The "founding team" framing means territory-building from zero with limited oversight, but the *mode* is unambiguously selling, not operating or engineering.

The €500k+ deal-size target is the defining number. It places this **an order of magnitude above Mark's current ~$50K ACV** and signals a true enterprise-strategic-sales motion (long cycles, large committees, procurement gauntlets, exec sponsorship) rather than the mid-market velocity motion most "AI startup AE" seats run.

## Fit notes for Mark

**Strong:**
- **Agentic-AI domain is dead-center.** Wonderful sells enterprise AI agents across voice/chat/email/back-office; Mark sells agentic enterprise IT decision-making at [[entities/stackgini]] and has personally shipped three production AI systems on raw LLM APIs ([[skills/ai-powered-sales-tooling]]). The "bridge GenAI experiments to Agentic AI outcomes" pitch is one he can deliver from genuine builder credibility, not buzzword fluency. Directly hits the "translate technical capabilities into business value" requirement — the engineer-seller thesis ([[concepts/founding-gtm-engineer-thesis]]) is exactly this.
- **Founding-team / early-stage-from-zero is his literal track record.** "Comfortable operating in early-stage, high-growth environments with limited oversight" + "building a territory or vertical from an early stage" (nice-to-have) describe what Mark did at Stackgini: first non-founder hire, built the GTM function from zero, $50K → $1M+ ARR. The highest-confidence match in the JD.
- **Complex multi-stakeholder enterprise deals.** Mark closed 25 enterprise accounts (DHL, Siemens Healthineers, REWE, Danaher, HAYS, National Bank of Liechtenstein) at 30% win rate over ~6-month cycles — genuinely complex, committee-driven enterprise sales. The multi-stakeholder buying-cycle requirement is met (note the intra-IT framing: [[feedback_stackgini_buying_centers]]).
- **German + English at executive level.** Hard requirement, fully met (Berlin-based, native-level German).
- **Partner-led deals — partial real reps.** "Proven success working with System Integrators and Hyperscalers on joint enterprise deals" is a *required* line. Mark sourced Danaher (F500, US) via the **SAP LeanIX partnership** — a concrete partner-sourced enterprise win. That is a real, citable instance of partner-led closing, even if not at the SI/Hyperscaler scale the JD implies. ^[inferred]
- **Hunter mentality, IC throughout.** Mark has been a hands-on IC seller his entire tenure (SDR → AE), with a record of generating and closing net-new — exactly the hunter profile, not a farmer or manager.

**Stretch:**
- **The €500k+ deal size is a ~10x ACV jump.** Mark's evidence base is $50K ACV deals. €500k+ enterprise-strategic deals are a different motion: longer cycles, larger committees, C-suite economic buyers, heavier procurement/legal, and multi-quarter forecasting. He has the *shape* of complex enterprise selling but not the *magnitude*. This is the single biggest risk axis, and a record of six-figure (let alone €500k) closes is implicitly what "consistently exceeding targets closing large deals" screens for. ^[inferred]
- **CxO / C-level relationships at large enterprises.** The JD stresses CxO and C-suite relationships repeatedly. Mark's enterprise reps are real but skew toward **intra-IT buying centers** (EA, InfoSec, ITAM) rather than the CEO/CFO/COO economic-buyer level ([[feedback_stackgini_buying_centers]]). IT leaders, yes; broad C-suite, thinner. The "first six-figure deal closed solo" is the strongest counter-evidence but the C-level-relationship bar reads senior. ^[inferred]
- **"Several years consistently exceeding targets" reads senior.** "Senior sales professionals" + "several years" + €500k deals points at a seasoned enterprise AE with 5+ years and a quota-attainment track record. Mark's full-cycle AE tenure is ~1.5 years (SDR→AE transition late 2024/early 2025); the founding-operator depth is strong but the *pure-closing seniority* is light against the implied bar.
- **SI / Hyperscaler "proven success" at scale.** The LeanIX-sourced Danaher win is one data point; the JD wants demonstrated, repeated joint-deal motion with SIs (Accenture/Deloitte-tier) and Hyperscalers (AWS/Azure/GCP marketplaces and co-sell). Mark has the seed of this, not a portfolio.
- **Geography: Austria territory, Berlin-based.** The seat carries the Austrian market, requires travel to Austrian customers, and is framed as the "local founding team" in Austria. Mark is in Berlin. Whether a Berlin base with travel is acceptable, or relocation/Austrian presence is expected, is the load-bearing logistics question. (Listed "Remote" but "Austria, Hybrid" in the title and "travel to customers in Austria" suggest in-territory presence is preferred.) ^[ambiguous]
- **Salesforce vs HubSpot.** The JD names Salesforce for account data. Mark's stack is HubSpot + custom scripts ([[project_stackgini_gtm_stack]]). Learnable, low-weight, but a tooling-switch.

**Calibration** (per [[synthesis/mark-progression-over-direction]]): roughly **~95% commercial / ~5% off**. This is the most commercial-pure seat tracked alongside [[references/fonio-gtm-engineer-jd|fonio]] — the entire remit is closing revenue. On progression terms it is a *deepening* move (further into enterprise closing, higher ACV, founding-equity upside) rather than a *broadening* one. It compounds GTM domain depth maximally but does not generalize the way [[references/remote-com-gtm-strategy-jd|Remote's strategy seat]] or [[references/enpulsion-ceo-associate-vienna-jd|ENPULSION CoS]] would. Good progression on *altitude of deal* and *equity*, neutral-to-narrow on *breadth of scope*.

**Interview-odds estimate**: ~15-25% to first screen. The founding-from-zero + agentic-AI-builder + German-at-exec-level combination is a genuinely differentiated profile, and an early-stage founding team often weights agency and AI-nativeness over pedigree. But the explicit bar — "senior sales professional," "several years exceeding targets," "€500k+ deals," "proven SI/Hyperscaler success" — screens for a seasoned enterprise closer with a heavy-ACV track record, which is precisely where Mark's evidence is lightest. The gap is not domain or hunger; it is *demonstrated large-deal seniority*. Net: a credible stretch-up application where the founding-operator-who-builds-AI angle has to outweigh a thinner pure-closing résumé.

## Application angle if pursued

Differentiator vs typical applicants (seasoned enterprise AEs with deep large-deal résumés but shallow AI-builder credibility):

1. **Lead with builder-credibility on agentic AI, not just sales pedigree.** Wonderful's whole pitch is "bridge GenAI experiments to Agentic AI outcomes." Mark is rare among enterprise sellers in that he has *built and shipped* production agentic AI on raw LLM APIs ([[skills/ai-powered-sales-tooling]], [[project_stackgini_gtm_stack]]). For an agentic-AI company, "I close the deal *and* I can speak to the architecture credibly because I build it" is a wedge a pure-closer competitor cannot match. This is the engineer-seller thesis as a selling advantage.
2. **Frame Stackgini as founding-from-zero enterprise closing.** Not "I was an AE," but "I was the first GTM hire, built the function from zero, and personally closed F500/enterprise logos (DHL, Siemens Healthineers, Danaher) into intra-IT committees over 6-month cycles." This maps directly onto "build a territory from an early stage," "limited oversight," and "complex multi-stakeholder buying cycles."
3. **Surface the partner-led win explicitly.** The Danaher deal sourced via the **SAP LeanIX partnership** is the citable answer to the "proven success with SIs/Hyperscalers" requirement. Lead with it on that line rather than leaving the requirement unaddressed.
4. **Address the deal-size gap proactively, on trajectory not parity.** Don't claim €500k closes that don't exist ([[feedback_no_self_hedging]] cuts both ways — state real reps as direct claims, but don't fabricate magnitude). Frame the first solo six-figure close and the F500 motion as the *on-ramp* to enterprise-strategic deal sizes, and lean on agentic-AI domain + founding-agency as why the ACV step-up is credible here specifically.
5. **Why-Wonderful narrative**: (a) agentic AI is what I already sell *and* build — this is the deepest possible domain match; (b) founding-team-from-zero is my proven environment; (c) the deal-altitude step-up (€500k+, equity) is the progression I'm optimizing for per [[synthesis/mark-progression-over-direction]]. Avoid generic "I love sales" energy; anchor on the AI-operator-who-closes intersection.

**Counter-signals to weigh before applying:**
- **The €500k+ / senior-closer bar is the real filter**, and large-deal seniority is Mark's weakest axis. If the screen hard-gates on demonstrated heavy-ACV attainment, the founding-operator angle may not clear it. Worth being honest that this is a stretch-up application.
- **Austria geography is unresolved.** Berlin base + travel vs. relocation/in-territory presence materially changes the opportunity. Clarify early.
- **No comp band published.** "Great salary + commission + early-stage shares" is unquantified; for a pure-commission-leveraged closer seat, the OTE split and equity terms are load-bearing for the opportunity-cost-vs-Stackgini math (and Stackgini equity is a direct opportunity cost per [[synthesis/mark-job-direction-map]] Category 2).
- **"Founding team" + "limited oversight" + carrying a number from zero** is high-variance: huge upside if the company is the "fastest-growing in Europe" as claimed, but founding-AE seats at unproven-traction startups can be brutal if the product or territory isn't ready. Company stage/traction is unstated and load-bearing.
