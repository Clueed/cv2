## Job Description (verbatim)

> **Revenue Operations & Business Development Manager (f/m/d)**
> On-site, Hybrid — NXAI GmbH (Linz, Oberösterreich, Austria) — Business
>
> Based in Linz, Austria, NXAI is a leading center for European AI innovation, turning advanced research into real-world industrial applications. Focused on next-generation xLSTM architectures, we offer companies a sovereign, on-premise alternative to US hyperscalers. A prime example is our zero-shot time series foundation model, TiRex, which delivers industrial enterprises a true technological edge - operating up to 50× more efficiently than traditional transformer models.
>
> As we now scale and build our commercial engine, we are looking for motivated people who want to join us on this journey and actively shape the future of AI with full commitment.
>
> Within this role you are the operational backbone of the Sales Team. You ensure that pipeline, processes and partner communication run smoothly and free up the CRO and AEs from everything that isn't a direct customer conversation. You always think one step ahead: what does the team need tomorrow to close deals today?
>
> **Your Key Responsibilities**
>
> - **Market Intelligence & Lead Generation** by conducting systematic market analysis and lead research to expand our target account list based on ICP criteria (Industry 4.0, Robotics, Energy).
> - **Revenue Ops & CRM Management** by building and maintaining our CRM structure (HubSpot), ensuring pipeline hygiene, reporting dashboards and well-documented GTM playbooks.
> - **Outreach & Content Preparation** by creating high-impact, personalized sales materials, including email sequences, use-case one-pagers and strategic account briefings.
> - **Commercial Support** by assisting in the preparation of proposals, structuring commercial contract documents in close coordination with the CRO and coordinating partner meetings.
> - **Cross-Functional Interface** by acting as the central bridge between the Sales Team and Tech Teams to ensure smooth internal communication.
> - **Project & PoC-tracking** by monitoring the progress of pilots and PoCs while coordinating key milestones and deadlines between customers and our Tech Teams.
>
> **What We Ask**
>
> - A strong technical and commercial hybrid profile with a degree in Business Informatics, Engineering, or a proven track record in a technical B2B environment (software, AI, data), enabling you to translate complex tech into clear business language.
> - A minimum of 3-5 years of professional experience in sales operations, business development, or a Go-To-Market function within a B2B tech or consulting environment, demonstrating deep familiarity with complex commercial workflows.
> - A data-driven operations expert with strong proficiency in CRM systems (preferably HubSpot) and modern productivity tools, skilled at creating commercial materials such as structured proposals, case studies and pricing documents.
> - An execution-focused process master with a structured, self-driven working style who brings order to ambiguity and actively utilizes generative AI tools as a standard, daily professional instrument.
> - A collaborative, outcome-driven professional who thrives on taking full ownership of results, approaches challenges with absolute pragmatism and puts collective success first to build Europe's AI champion together.
> - A proactive self-starter with deep customer empathy and fluent German and English skills (negotiation level), who shares curiosity for industrial AI and B2B ecosystems.
>
> **What You'll Get**
>
> - **Attractive Compensation**: A competitive base salary starting from EUR 4,643 and a significant variable component based on a strong OTE logic.
> - **Financial Upside (VSOP)**: Participation in our Virtual Stock Option Plan (VSOP).
> - **Mobility Support**: We fully cover either your Klimaticket (public transport) or your parking spot.
> - **Founding Role & Tailored Growth Path**: A unique position in the commercial team of one of Europe's most exciting AI companies, giving you the chance to shape processes from the ground up. We will co-create and transparently align your career path and scale your role as we build our commercial engine.
> - **Direct Access & Impact**: A direct line to our CRO and executive management — zero layers, zero bureaucracy, maximum speed.
> - **True Co-Creation**: When defining our GTM strategies, pricing models and sales playbooks — here, you build the future instead of just executing it.
> - **Flexibility**: Remote and hybrid work options tailored to your lifestyle.
> - **Relocation Support**: We will support you through the entire relocation and visa process.
> - **Add-On**: Access to international tech conferences, regular team events and a weekly onsite team lunch.

## What this role actually is

Weighted reading of the JD:

| Pillar | Weight | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| Market intelligence & ICP lead generation | ~25% | "Systematic market analysis and lead research to expand our target account list based on ICP criteria (Industry 4.0, Robotics, Energy)." Build and feed the target-account engine. This is the load-bearing top-of-funnel pillar. |
| Revenue Ops & CRM (HubSpot) | ~25% | "Building and maintaining our CRM structure (HubSpot), pipeline hygiene, reporting dashboards, well-documented GTM playbooks." The RevOps core: own the system of record and the operating instrumentation. |
| Outreach & content preparation | ~20% | "Email sequences, use-case one-pagers, strategic account briefings." Sales-material and sequence production — enablement, not closing. |
| Commercial support | ~15% | "Preparation of proposals, structuring commercial contract documents in coordination with the CRO, coordinating partner meetings." Deal-desk-adjacent support. |
| Cross-functional interface (Sales ↔ Tech) | ~10% | Central bridge between the Sales Team and Tech Teams; internal comms. |
| Project & PoC tracking | ~5% | Monitor pilots/PoCs, coordinate milestones/deadlines between customers and Tech Teams. |

Effective role: a **RevOps + Business Development operator** who is the "operational backbone of the Sales Team" — builds the CRM/dashboards/playbooks, runs ICP-based lead generation, produces the outreach and commercial materials, and coordinates PoCs, so the CRO and AEs can spend time in front of customers. It is explicitly **not a quota-carrying closing seat** (the whole framing is "everything that isn't a direct customer conversation"), and it is explicitly a **builder** seat ("you build the future instead of just executing it," "shape processes from the ground up").

This is a **hybrid commercial/off-commercial** seat, tilted commercial: the *content* is entirely GTM (lead gen, pipeline, outreach, proposals) but the *mode* is operations, systems-building, and enablement rather than selling. Structurally it is a **GTM-Engineering / RevOps seat wearing a "BizDev Manager" title** — the closest tracked cousins are the [[references/fonio-gtm-engineer-jd|fonio]] and [[references/nuitee-gtm-engineer-jd|Nuitée]] GTM-Engineer seats and the [[references/lago-growth-chief-of-staff-jd|Lago Growth CoS]], minus the founder-office autonomy and CEO reporting line (here the reporting line is the CRO, and the framing is *support the sales team* rather than *own growth*).
