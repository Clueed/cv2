## What this role actually is

Weighted reading of the JD:

| Pillar | Weight | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| Target & market research | ~35% | Independent research/analysis on target markets and potential company targets, aligned to group strategy. The core of "deal sourcing" — building the funnel of who to acquire. |
| Target evaluation → recommendation | ~25% | Assess identified targets, derive actionable recommendations ("Handlungsempfehlungen") for the group. Where analytical judgement + financial literacy are priced in. |
| Reporting & management communication | ~20% | Produce reports and internal presentations; make complex topics "managementtauglich" (management-ready). Deck-craft + narrative for Top-Management and operating Business Units. |
| Cross-functional project coordination | ~15% | Coordinate internal experts across the STRABAG group + external advisors/consultants in interdisciplinary project teams. Influence-without-authority, internally. |
| Scouting/research tooling | ~5% | Apply current scouting and research tools. Small in the JD's weighting but the **strongest single bridge to Mark's built work** (see below). |

Effective role: **an M&A deal-sourcing / corporate-development analyst** — build and qualify the acquisition/partnership target funnel, evaluate targets, and package recommendations for the top-management decision layer. No commercial pillar (no selling, no revenue ownership). No people management. It is a research-and-analysis seat inside a large-cap conglomerate's corp-dev function.

This is the closest tracked seat to [[references/first-momentum-visiting-associate-2027-jd|the FMV Visiting Associate]] in *mechanics* (sourcing + research + target evaluation + recommendations), but the differences are large: **corporate M&A** (not venture), **permanent** (not fixed-term), and at a **74K-person construction conglomerate** (not a lean deep-tech VC).

## Fit notes for Mark

"Analysierst und interpretierst Daten und leitest daraus Handlungsempfehlungen ab" is exactly the thesis shape: pull primary data, analyze, conclude.

Most corp-dev analysts have never *built* a company-screening system; Mark has shipped one. This is the differentiator.
- **Founder-side deal mechanics** — Stackgini fundraising-support reps (data-room governance, commercial DD Q&A, cap-table/dilution discussions, term-sheet review — [[entities/mark-vavulov]]) are legitimate deal-process exposure. **Stretch:**

Mark has ~2.5 years, clearing the *duration*, but in a **commercial founding-GTM role, not an analytical/deal seat**. His analytical reps are the IFRS thesis, [[references/tendenz-project|tendenz]] (z-score equity-significance screening tool), fundraising commercial-DD, and the ICP-scoring build — real, but not IB/consulting deal-analysis reps. The pool (52% senior, IB/consulting/MSc-heavy per the clipping) will out-rep him on classic M&A analysis. **This is the hard gate.**
- **Zero M&A-execution and construction-sector reps** — no acquisition-target valuation or construction-industry experience. Construction is "von Vorteil" (a plus, not required), so the sector gap is survivable; the M&A-analysis gap is the pointier one.
- **Finance-claims discipline** — per [[feedback_finance_claims_scope]]: claim the degree, the founder-side deal mechanics, and the Excel/modeling toolkit freely; **do not overclaim M&A-target-valuation reps, LP-side work, or building a full model from scratch.** For a corp-dev seat the honest frame is "quantitative-analysis + company-screening builder with founder-side deal exposure," not "M&A analyst."
- **Big-company, slow-cadence environment** — this is a 74K-person listed conglomerate, the opposite end of the spectrum from the startups/scaleups/frontier labs Mark has targeted. The direction map ([[synthesis/mark-job-direction-map]]) explicitly flags "big-company politics" as an unresolved dealbreaker. Corp-dev sits above the bureaucracy, but the ambient pace and construction domain are a real culture question.
- **No commercial pillar** — like [[references/enpulsion-ceo-associate-vienna-jd|ENPULSION]], this seat stops compounding Mark's engineer-seller leverage directly. The progression case is "corp-dev / deal-analytics reps + large-cap brand," not commercial deepening.
- **Comp undisclosed** — no band in the clipping; Austrian analyst corp-dev comp is likely modest vs. Vienna AE OTE ceilings. ^[inferred]

**Calibration** (per [[synthesis/mark-progression-over-direction]]): roughly **~10-15% commercial / ~85-90% off-commercial** — a finance/analytical *mode*, neither selling nor systems-building for revenue. Comparable off-commercial depth to [[references/enpulsion-ceo-associate-vienna-jd|ENPULSION CoS]] (~15-20% commercial) and more off-commercial than [[references/allocator-one-investment-manager-vienna-jd|Allocator One IR]] (~70%) or [[references/remote-com-gtm-strategy-jd|Remote GTM Strategy]] (~75%). Unlike the CoS seat, the *content* is finance/deal-analysis rather than execution-coordination — it leans on the education atoms ([[references/mark-education]]) the way Allocator One did, not the operator atoms.

The progression question: is the **corp-dev analyst seat at month 13** more valuable than the month-13 seat from a commercial next step (founding GTM Engineer, PMM, Growth Generalist)? Corp-dev at a large cap can ladder to strategy/M&A/BizOps roles and carries a recognizable brand, but it steps *off* the compounding engineer-seller track into a domain (construction M&A) far from Mark's AI/SaaS base. Per the progression filter this is a genuine directional swing, defensible only if the corp-dev/deal-analytics reps are the direction Mark wants to option into.

## Application angle if pursued

1. **Lead with the scouting engine as a target-identification system** — open on the Europe-wide ICP scoring database ([[skills/ai-powered-sales-tooling]]): a system that identifies, scores, and ranks companies across Europe against a strategic profile from SERP/firmographics/AI-research signals. Frame it explicitly as the same machine target scouting needs, already built and shipped. This is the one thing no consulting-analyst applicant can match.
2. **Anchor the analytical credential on the quantitative thesis** — the IFRS R&D-capitalization thesis (primary filings, ~300 firm-year obs, regression) is the cleanest proof of "analysiert und interpretiert Daten und leitet Handlungsempfehlungen ab" outside a deal seat. Pair with the CBS Financial Management modeling toolkit.
3. **Bridge founder-side deal mechanics honestly** — data-room governance, commercial DD Q&A, cap-table/term-sheet exposure as real deal-process reps, without inflating them into M&A-execution or valuation reps he doesn't have ([[feedback_finance_claims_scope]], [[feedback_no_self_hedging]]).
4. **Cross-functional coordination** — reframe intra-IT buying-center orchestration ([[feedback_stackgini_buying_centers]]) as coordinating internal experts + external advisors across a group.
5. **Why-STRABAG / why-construction-M&A** — needs a real answer, not generic curiosity. The most honest angle is the **"construction technology" scouting mandate**: STRABAG's corp-dev explicitly scouts technology trends and innovative technologies/products, not only traditional construction bolt-ons — that tech-scouting slice is where an AI-native builder who scores companies for a living is genuinely additive. Land the why on that overlap, not on the construction domain.

**Counter-signals to weigh before applying:**
- The analytical-deal-reps gap is real and the pool is targeted; without the scouting-engine hook this is a low-probability application.
- The seat steps off the engineer-seller compounding track into construction M&A — only worth it if corp-dev/deal-analytics is a direction Mark actively wants to option.
- Big-company cadence and construction domain are the furthest cultural distance from his targets to date.
