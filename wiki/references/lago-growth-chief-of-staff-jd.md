# Lago — Growth Chief of Staff (Remote Europe)

**Title posted**: Growth Chief of Staff
**Company**: [[entities/lago|Lago]] (open-source billing / usage-based-pricing platform)
**Location**: Remote Europe (applicants must be based in Europe; other locations not considered). Fully remote with "frequent trips to the US."
**Type**: Full-time / permanent
**Team**: GTM
**Reporting line**: Directly to the **CEO**
**Comp**: Competitive salary + stock options (no band disclosed)
**Source**: Lago careers page, clipping pasted into the [[projects/cv2/cv2|cv2]] session on 2026-07-08

This page captures the JD verbatim and analyzes it against Mark's filter ([[synthesis/mark-progression-over-direction]]) and the role typology ([[synthesis/mark-job-direction-map]]). It is a **Founder's-Office / Growth-CoS owner seat** — the cleanest structural match tracked so far to what Mark actually did at [[entities/stackgini|Stackgini]] (build the GTM function *and* its tooling from a blank page), wrapped in a Chief-of-Staff title and pointed at growth/ops. Company context lives in [[entities/lago]].

## Job Description (verbatim)

> **About Lago**
>
> At Lago, we're on a mission to make Monetization simpler for tech companies.
>
> As the leading open-source billing platform, we help businesses build flexible pricing models and scale with confidence. We've raised over $22 million in funding from top-tier investors including Y Combinator and FirstMark.
>
> Our platform has garnered around 10K stars on GitHub, a testament to our active and growing developer community. Companies such as PayPal, CoreWeave, Synthesia, and Mistral.ai choose Lago for their billing. And we have barely spent any money on Marketing or Ads so far.
>
> We're a team of ambitious, focused individuals who solve hard problems with excellence and accountability. At Lago, we value reliability and proactivity. We trust each other to own tasks, drive them to completion, and continuously improve them. While we encourage you to try fast and "fail fast", we also expect you to learn fast: make mistakes, but don't make the same one twice.
>
> If you're excited by challenges and want to grow alongside a high-performing, supportive team, this is the place for you.
>
> **Overview**
>
> We are looking for a Growth Chief of Staff to take founder-defined priorities and turn them into shipped systems. Someone who can operate across GTM, ops, and growth without needing a playbook handed to them.
>
> This is not a fixed-scope role. Priorities shift. New problems emerge. What does not change is the expectation: you see what needs doing, own it, ship it, and turn it into a self-improving system.
>
> **What You'll Be Doing** — Examples of projects:
>
> - Build and own account outreach and nurturing motions from scratch
> - Improve website conversion and CRM handoff processes
> - Manage press, social, and brand projects around founder-led priorities
> - Build dashboards, alerts, and operating cadences that keep the team moving, accountable, and improving
> - Turn founder-led motions into campaigns, workflows, and repeatable playbooks
> - Use AI tools and automation, while keeping humans in the loop when necessary
>
> **Who You Are**
>
> You take the owner seat. When a founder drops a half-formed idea, you do not wait for a follow-up. You turn it into a plan, identify what is missing, and own the outcome.
>
> You thrive on shifting priorities. This role will change shape as Lago grows. You're not looking for a rigid job spec. You're energized by being handed a new problem, figuring out the approach from scratch, and driving the impact.
>
> You communicate before you're asked. You share what you're working on, where things stand, and when something isn't working. Proactively, without needing a check-in to prompt you.
>
> You're curious and hands-on about technical and AI products. You don't need to be an engineer. But when you're evaluating a company, you read the docs, try the product, and build prototypes or workflows to understand it deeply. You understand what an API, a webhook, or an MCP is, or you can figure out a new technical concept quickly and explain why a customer would use it. That curiosity and builder-first mindset are non-negotiable.
>
> You're ruthless about what actually matters. You can look at five possible projects and correctly identify the one that moves the needle this week. You don't confuse activity with impact.
>
> **Who You're Not**
>
> - Someone who arrives wanting to audit everything and start fresh. The priority is to double down on what already works.
> - A strategist who produces decks. You produce shipped things.
> - Someone focused on inputs. Outputs and impact are the north stars.
> - Someone easily overwhelmed by things they have never done before or technical concepts. It has never been easier to learn new skills.
>
> **Requirements**
>
> - 5+ years of experience in Growth Ops, GTM Ops, RevOps, BizOps, or a Founder's Office role with genuine GTM ownership
> - Proven track record of building workflows, dashboards, and operating cadences, not just coordinating them
> - Experience breaking down undefined problems: taking a business need and turning it into prioritized workstreams, aligned teams, clear steps, shipped outcomes, and feedback loops
> - Comfortable with CRM, automation, and project management tooling, and curious enough to figure out what you haven't used before
> - Enough marketing and business intuition to understand audience, timing, and messaging
>
> **Nice to have**
>
> - Experience in B2B SaaS, developer tools, or technical infrastructure environments
> - Exposure to API-driven products, usage-based pricing, or billing business models
> - French language skills
>
> **Why Join Lago?**
>
> - Work with top-tier international talent (12+ nationalities; "slope over intercept").
> - Shape the future of monetization and fintech ("Monetization systems are shifting with AI").
> - Fully remote with frequent trips to the US; work from anywhere in Europe.
> - Competitive salary and stock options.
> - Reporting directly to the CEO; work shapes how customers like Mistral, Groq, Synthesia, and enterprises monetize.
>
> (Applicants must be based in Europe for this role.)

## What this role actually is

Weighted reading of the JD:

| Pillar | Weight | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| Build GTM/growth systems from scratch | ~40% | "Build and own account outreach and nurturing motions from scratch"; "turn founder-led motions into campaigns, workflows, and repeatable playbooks." Build the machine, not run a fixed playbook. This is the load-bearing pillar. |
| Ops instrumentation & cadence | ~20% | "Build dashboards, alerts, and operating cadences that keep the team moving, accountable, and improving." RevOps/BizOps instrumentation and the operating rhythm. |
| Growth-marketing execution | ~20% | Website conversion, CRM handoff, press/social/brand projects around founder priorities. Demand-adjacent, but execution-owned rather than campaign-managed. |
| Prioritization / owner judgement | ~15% | "Take founder-defined priorities and turn them into shipped systems"; "ruthless about what actually matters." The Chief-of-Staff core: convert half-formed founder ideas into owned outcomes. |
| AI-native automation | ~5% (pervasive) | Explicitly "use AI tools and automation, keeping humans in the loop." Small as a line item but framed as the default operating mode across every pillar. |

Effective role: **a Founder's-Office GTM/growth-ops owner** — reporting to the CEO, take shifting founder-defined priorities and ship self-improving systems across GTM, ops, and growth. Not a quota-carrying seat (no direct revenue number, no closing pillar) and not a "produces decks" strategist ("you produce shipped things"). The explicit anti-pattern is the deck-monkey Chief of Staff — this JD hard-codes the **builder/operator** shape and rejects the coordinator shape ("building… not just coordinating them"). That is the exact distinction the [[references/enpulsion-ceo-associate-vienna-jd|ENPULSION CoS]] analysis flagged as the green-flag-vs-yellow-flag fork; Lago's JD lands firmly on the green flag.

This is a **hybrid commercial/off-commercial** seat. The content is growth and GTM (commercial family), but the *mode* is systems-building and ops, not selling. It is the closest tracked seat to Mark's literal day job — build the GTM function AND its tooling from a blank page ([[references/nuitee-application-2026-06-28|the Nuitée package's own framing]]).

## Fit notes for Mark

**Strong (this is close to a spec of Mark's actual work):**
- **"Build GTM motions from scratch" = his Stackgini job description.** Mark built the entire GTM function from zero at [[entities/stackgini|Stackgini]] (~$20K → $1.25M ARR) — outbound, nurturing, CRM, the operating cadence, the SDR ramp. "Build and own account outreach and nurturing motions from scratch" is not a stretch reframe; it is what he did. This is the strongest structural match of any seat tracked.
- **"Building workflows, dashboards, operating cadences, not just coordinating them"** — the requirement explicitly privileges builders over coordinators, and Mark's evidence is *shipped systems*: the Europe-wide ICP scoring database, the account-research agent, and the cold-email infra built as a Claude Code workspace ([[skills/ai-powered-sales-tooling]]). He clears the exact bar the JD uses to filter out consultant-CoS applicants.
- **AI-native, humans-in-the-loop, on raw APIs** — "use AI tools and automation, keeping humans in the loop" is Mark's default mode. He builds on raw LLM APIs (OpenAI/Claude/Gemini) and is skeptical of low-code ([[concepts/founding-gtm-engineer-thesis]]). This is a first-class hiring signal here, not a bonus.
- **Dev-tools / API / webhook / MCP literacy is genuinely non-negotiable and genuinely met** — "you understand what an API, a webhook, or an MCP is." Mark integrates HubSpot/Apollo/Lusha via APIs, orchestrates LLM tool-calls, and works with MCP servers directly. The "read the docs, try the product, build a prototype to understand it" standard is how he already operates. Very few growth-ops applicants clear the MCP bar; Mark clears it comfortably.
- **Owner-seat / half-formed-idea → shipped outcome** — founding-from-zero at a two-person-to-$1.25M motion is the strongest possible evidence of taking ambiguity and owning the outcome without a playbook. "Founder's Office role with genuine GTM ownership" is in the requirements list *as an accepted background*, and Mark's is a real one.
- **B2B SaaS + dev-tools + API-driven / usage-based / billing (all three nice-to-haves)** — Stackgini is B2B SaaS sold to technical (IT) buyers; Mark's whole tooling stack is API-driven. Usage-based-pricing / billing is not a domain he has worked *in*, but the adjacent AI-infra logo set (Mistral, Groq, CoreWeave) is exactly his AI/SaaS base, and metered billing is intuitive to an engineer-seller.
- **Proactive communication** — the JD's "communicate before you're asked" maps onto a founding operator's default cadence with founders/CRO/CEO (Mark ran quarterly cadence with CRO/CEO).
- **Remote Europe, Berlin-based** — Mark is Berlin-based; he clears the "must be based in Europe" gate cleanly. No relocation, unlike the frontier-lab seats.

**Stretch:**
- **"5+ years" vs. ~2.5 years is the binding gate.** The requirement is 5+ years in Growth Ops / GTM Ops / RevOps / BizOps / Founder's Office with genuine GTM ownership. Mark has ~2.5 years — real, dense, from-zero ownership, but roughly half the stated floor. This is the same experience-depth gap that makes [[references/anthropic-gtm-strategy-ops-dach-jd|Anthropic GTM S&O]] (10+) a stretch, though far less severe here. The offsetting argument is depth-over-tenure: he *built* the function others would have 5 years of *operating*.
- **Growth-marketing breadth (press/social/brand/website conversion) is his thinner half** — Mark's reps are outbound/ABM/RevOps-engineering and enterprise selling, not brand/PR/social or conversion-rate optimization. He can credibly own website→CRM handoff and campaign systemization; press/social/brand is the part he'd be learning on the job. The JD's "figure out what you haven't used before" framing tolerates this, but it is the real skill gap.
- **PLG / self-serve / community-led growth motion** — Lago's growth is open-source-community- and product-led (10K GitHub stars, "barely spent on marketing"), closer to the [[references/nuitee-gtm-engineer-jd|Nuitée PLG variant]] than to Stackgini's enterprise-outbound motion. Mark's from-zero build was outbound/ABM-led into enterprise IT; the community/dev-adoption flywheel is a different growth physics he has studied (OSS-incentives thesis surfaced for [[references/vendure-application-2026-06-03|Vendure]]) but not run.
- **No closing / quota pillar** — like the CoS seats, this stops compounding Mark's *enterprise-closing* leverage directly. But unlike [[references/enpulsion-ceo-associate-vienna-jd|ENPULSION]], it keeps compounding his GTM-systems-building leverage, which is the more durable half of the profile. Net: less of a directional swing than any CoS seat tracked.
- **French language (nice-to-have)** — Mark is a German native (English fluent), not a French speaker. It is explicitly a nice-to-have, so not a gate, but Lago reads France-rooted ([[entities/lago]]) and French may be an ambient advantage he lacks. ^[inferred]

**Calibration** (per [[synthesis/mark-progression-over-direction]]): roughly **~50-60% commercial / ~40-50% off-commercial** — a growth/GTM-ops *owner* seat where the content is commercial (outreach, conversion, campaigns, CRM) but the mode is systems-building and ops. More commercial than [[references/enpulsion-ceo-associate-vienna-jd|ENPULSION CoS]] (~15-20%) or [[references/strabag-target-scouting-analyst-vienna-jd|STRABAG]] (~10-15%); less purely commercial than [[references/wonderful-gtm-austria-jd|Wonderful]] (pure closer) or [[references/elevenlabs-growth-generalist-jd|ElevenLabs Growth Generalist]] (~90%). Closest in *shape* to the [[references/fonio-gtm-engineer-jd|GTM Engineer]] / [[references/nuitee-gtm-engineer-jd|GTM-Eng]] seats (build the machine) but wrapped in a Founder's-Office/CoS title with a broader ops+brand surface.

Under the progression filter this is a **strong next-step candidate**: it compounds the most durable half of Mark's profile (GTM-systems-building, AI-native tooling) rather than trading it away, keeps him in his AI/SaaS/dev-tools base, reports directly to a CEO (scope + signal), and carries YC/FirstMark brand + equity. The month-13 seat here — "built and owned the growth engine at a YC-backed OSS billing platform, reporting to the CEO" — is a clean progression story with GM/COO/founder exit ramps, the good-CoS trajectory.

## Interview-odds estimate

**~20-30% to first screen.** ^[inferred] The structural match is unusually high — this JD reads almost like a description of Mark's Stackgini work, and the two non-negotiables (builder-first mindset + API/webhook/MCP literacy) are exactly where he separates from a generic growth-ops applicant. The binding constraint is the **5+ years** floor against ~2.5, and the growth-marketing/PLG breadth gap; a remote-Europe YC-backed role will draw a deep pool including 5-8-year RevOps/BizOps operators. The realistic path is a founder/CEO who indexes on *shipped systems + AI-native building + owner mindset* over year-count — which this JD's language ("outputs and impact are the north stars," "it has never been easier to learn new skills") explicitly invites. If the screen is run by a recruiter filtering on the "5+ years" line, odds drop toward the low end.

## Application angle if pursued

1. **Lead with "I built the GTM machine and its tooling from a blank page."** Open on the from-zero Stackgini build ($20K → $1.25M): the outbound/nurturing motions, the CRM/cadence, the SDR ramp — *and* the systems underneath them (ICP-scoring DB, account-research agent, cold-email infra as a Claude Code workspace, [[skills/ai-powered-sales-tooling]]). This is the "shipped systems, not decks" proof the JD screens for, told as one story.
2. **Show, don't tell, the AI-native + API/MCP literacy.** The JD says "read the docs, try the product, build a prototype." Do it: try Lago (it's open source), and reference a concrete integration/prototype instinct (e.g., how you'd instrument usage-based billing events into a growth dashboard, or wire a webhook → CRM handoff). Naming MCP/webhooks accurately is a cheap, high-signal differentiator against the growth-ops pool.
3. **Reframe the tenure gap as depth-over-year-count honestly.** ~2.5 years of *building a function from zero* is denser ownership than 5 years of operating inside one. State it as a direct claim ([[feedback_no_self_hedging]]) — "I built what most growth-ops hires inherit" — without inflating the years.
4. **Address the PLG/community-growth physics head-on.** Acknowledge his from-zero motion was enterprise-outbound, and connect to the OSS-incentives thinking already surfaced for [[references/vendure-application-2026-06-03|Vendure]]: dev-community-led growth is a distribution engine he's studied and is energized to build the paid/outbound layer *on top of* — "double down on what works" (their 10K stars), not "audit and start fresh" (the explicit anti-pattern).
5. **Why-Lago**: the honest anchor is **AI-native monetization** — usage-based/metered billing is how AI-infra products (Mistral, Groq, CoreWeave in their logo set) charge, and building the growth engine for the picks-and-shovels layer of the AI economy is a genuine progression bet for an AI-native GTM builder. Land the why there, not on billing as a domain.

**Counter-signals to weigh before applying:**
- The 5+ years floor is real; without a founder who over-indexes on shipped-systems evidence, the recruiter screen is the risk.
- Growth-marketing breadth (brand/press/social/CRO) and the community-led/PLG motion are genuine gaps vs. his outbound/enterprise base.
- No band disclosed; "competitive + options" at a Series-A-ish OSS company needs a real number before committing.
