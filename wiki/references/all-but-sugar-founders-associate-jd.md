# All But Sugar — Founder's Associate, Vienna

**Title posted**: Founders Associate
**Company**: [[entities/all-but-sugar|All But Sugar GmbH]] — modular platform + process technology for 1:1 sucrose-replacement sweetening systems
**Location**: Vienna, Austria (central, Hauptbahnhof)
**Type**: Angestellte/r (employee, full-time)
**Reports to**: The founder ("the founder's right hand")
**Comp**: €45,000-65,000 / year
**Languages**: Fluent English **and German** (both required)
**Tooling**: Google Drive, ClickUp, Pipedrive, Canva, **Claude**
**Recruiting contact**: Lisa Reiss
**Source**: Pasted verbatim by user 2026-07-16 from the join.com posting (Job ID 16427981)

This page captures the JD verbatim and analyzes it against Mark's filter ([[synthesis/mark-progression-over-direction]]). The seat is a **do-everything founder's right-hand** role at an early-stage Vienna food-tech — the **third verified Founder's-Associate JD** in the wiki (after [[references/enpulsion-ceo-associate-vienna-jd|ENPULSION]] and [[references/biolyz-founders-associate-jd|Biolyz]]), and the **first one with a real commercial + finance + operations execution mandate** rather than a coordination/analysis CoS shape.

## Job Description (verbatim)

> **All But Sugar GmbH — Founders Associate**
> Wien, Österreich · Angestellte/r · Business · 45.000 € bis 65.000 € / Jahr
>
> All But Sugar is building the first modular platform and process technology for sugar alternatives that replace sucrose 1:1, no sugar, no synthetic sweeteners, high in fiber, reduced calories, and EFSA-compliant. Our goal: first European, then global market leader in full replacement sweetening systems.
>
> We win on two fronts at once: technology so far ahead that it sells itself, and a sales engine built to turn that edge into revenue at scale. Level up sweetness with us and grow the foundation to rewrite how the world eats.
>
> **Why this isn't a typical assistant job**
>
> You won't get a job description with a fixed task list, you'll get a company still being built. You're not assisting. You're executing, with ownership from day one. One day it's an investor update, the next a pricing conversation with a co-manufacturer, the day after a job interview you run yourself. Your motto: 'there's always a way. When something doesn't work, you try a different angle instead of giving up.'
>
> **Aufgaben**
>
> You're the founder's right hand, without routine, but insights into every part of the company. You work on daily to dos and own entire projects end-to-end. And because building the company also means selling it, a large part of the role is hands-on strategy and process implementation to make us more efficient and effective.
>
> Your Responsibilities
>
> - Identify new sales opportunities from distributors to brands
> - Pricing & calculations: contribute to margins, cost models, and business cases
> - Develop and execute go-to-market strategies
> - Contract management (NDAs, supplier and customer agreements) in coordination with lawyers, tax advisors, and mentors
> - Logistics, orders, deliveries & sample handling
> - Bookkeeping, cash flow calculation, and budgeting
> - Investor updates, fundraising support
> - Build and update Marketing material
> - Plan and submit grant applications
> - Prepare onboarding material, run first-round interviews
>
> **Qualifikation**
>
> Your profile
>
> - 2-4+ years of experience in business development, sales, consulting, operations, or finance, B2B, food, or health background as a plus
> - Just as comfortable building a financial plan as negotiating with a decision-maker and updating a website
> - Structured, analytical way of working with a strong results orientation
> - High degree of initiative, ownership, and customer orientation
> - Willing to teach yourself new technology fast, genuine interest in the product
> - Comfortable with Google Drive, ClickUp, Pipedrive, Canva, Claude
> - Fluent English and German
> - Willingness to travel
>
> **Benefits**
>
> What we offer
>
> - Ownership from day one
> - One of the steepest learning curves you'll find, spanning Tech, Operations, and Sales
> - A team that builds more than it talks
> - MacBook, Public Transport Ticket
> - Central location in Vienna (Hauptbahnhof)
> - Unlimited access to the best sugar-free candy
>
> **Our hiring process**
>
> 1. 15-20 minute intro call.
> 2. Skill interview: a case study sent 2 hours in advance shows how you actually work through a real problem.
> 3. Culture-fit conversation with the founder.
>
> Send us your CV and one sentence on why you're the right person for this role.

Effective role: **a true founder's-right-hand GENERALIST** — commercial + finance + ops + fundraising + a little legal/people, owned end-to-end, at a 1-10-person food-tech. No direct reports (yet), broad ownership. This is the **least "chief-of-staff-coordinator" and most "GM-in-training / mini-founder" of the three Founder's-Associate JDs**: it explicitly disavows "assisting" ("You're not assisting. You're executing"). The shape is closest to what Mark actually did at [[entities/stackgini|Stackgini]] — wearing every hat in a founding team — but pointed at a physical-goods food-tech instead of B2B SaaS. ^[inferred]

**Stretch:**
- **Physical-goods operations / logistics is a genuine new rep** — "logistics, orders, deliveries & sample handling" and "pricing conversation with a co-manufacturer" are a physical food supply chain. Mark's ops reps are RevOps / process / capacity modeling, not warehousing, freight, sample fulfilment, or co-manufacturer negotiation. The systems-thinking transfers; the domain mechanics don't. Learnable, but a real gap.
- **Food-tech / food-science / EFSA-regulatory domain has zero reps** — the vegan-interest bridge covers the *why-this-product* motivation but does nothing for food process technology, co-manufacturing, or EU novel-food / food-additive regulation. Lighter than Biolyz's wet-lab gap (this is food, not regulated diagnostics), but still a domain with no reps.
- **Comp is modest** — €45-65K is real and unambiguous but at the low end of the tracked set (below fonio €65-95K, Vendure €55-70K, NXAI ~€65K+variable+VSOP; comparable to the ENPULSION floor). For an early-stage founder's-associate seat this is expected; equity/upside is not mentioned in the JD (verify).
- **"Without routine," "steepest learning curve," "own entire projects end-to-end" at a 1-10-person company** — this is a very-early-stage, high-breadth, high-intensity seat. Not stated as bluntly as ENPULSION's "not a 9-to-5," but the ownership/intensity expectation is real. A genuine question of whether Mark wants mini-founder breadth at a pre-scale food-tech vs a deeper commercial/GTM seat at a larger AI company.
- **Pre-scale food-tech is a bet on a single company + market** — 1-10 FTE, product maturity unknown, "technology so far ahead it sells itself" is a claim to diligence, not a fact ([[entities/all-but-sugar]] needs research). The progression upside is real (mini-GM breadth, equity-shaped ownership) but the company risk is higher than at a funded scaleup.

**Calibration** (per [[synthesis/mark-progression-over-direction]]): roughly **~45-55% commercial / ~45-55% off-commercial** — a **true hybrid**, the most balanced of any role tracked. Commercial content (sales opportunities, GTM strategy, marketing) sits alongside heavy finance + ops + fundraising. This is more commercial than the other two Founder's-Associate seats (ENPULSION ~15-20%, Biolyz ~10-15%) and more balanced than Lago (~50-60% commercial but growth-ops-flavored) or NXAI (~60-70%). It is the **cleanest expression of the "progression over direction" thesis tracked**: it compounds Mark's *current* commercial expertise AND broadens him into finance/ops/GM in one seat, at a known geography, in his native language.

The progression question (per the synthesis): is the **seat at month 13** as a founder's right hand at a pre-scale Vienna food-tech more valuable than the month-13 seat from a deeper commercial role (next-tier AE, GTM Engineer, founding PMM)? This is the **green-flag "mini-founder / GM-in-training" shape** the other two FA JDs only gestured at: the JD hard-codes end-to-end ownership, explicitly disavows "assisting," and spans Tech/Ops/Sales. The bet is that breadth + ownership + the finance/ops/fundraising broadening is worth more than commercial depth for the next step. The company-risk and comp-ceiling are the counterweights.

1. **Founding operator, not assistant** — Mark has spent ~2.5 years as a founding team member wearing commercial, finance, ops, and fundraising hats simultaneously ($20K → $1.25M ARR, 1 → 35 customers, 1 → 10 FTEs). The JD's "you're not assisting, you're executing, with ownership from day one" is a description of what he already did. This is the single strongest wedge — lead with it.
2. **The tri-lateral line, answered verbatim** — "just as comfortable building a financial plan as negotiating with a decision-maker and updating a website" is answerable directly: financial plan (CBS Financial Management + IFRS thesis + fundraising models), negotiating (enterprise deals, F500 anchors), website/build (ships on raw LLM APIs). Hit all three; most applicants can only hit one or two.
3. **Finance is a center-of-role strength here, not a bridge** — anchor pricing/margins/cost-models/business-cases on the finance degree + Luther HGB Excel + founder-side deal mechanics. Hold the [[feedback_finance_claims_scope|claims-scope discipline]] (no waterfalls / LP-side / fund-model-from-scratch).
4. **Commercial + AI tooling compounds** — reframe the Europe-wide ICP scoring engine ([[skills/ai-powered-sales-tooling]]) as a distributor/brand opportunity-screening system, and note the company already runs on Claude (which he builds on daily). "Willing to teach yourself new technology fast" is show-don't-tell here.
5. **Why All But Sugar via the genuine vegan/health bridge** — Mark's real veganism + fitness interest is an authentic hook into better-for-you / sugar-reduction food. Land it as a real "I care about this product" story ([[feedback_no_self_hedging]] — state the interest directly, don't hedge, and don't fabricate food-science expertise).
