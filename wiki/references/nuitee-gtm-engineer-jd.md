## The PLG-vs-outbound axis (key distinction from fonio)

The most important structural fact about this seat: it is a **product-led-growth** GTM-Engineer role, not an **outbound** one.

- [[references/fonio-gtm-engineer-jd|fonio]] = build the *outbound machine* (Clay/Apollo/Instantly/Smartlead/PhantomBuster — cold-email at SMB volume, deliverability, sender rotation). The demand is *manufactured*.
- **Nuitée = build the *inbound/PLG machine*** (PostHog + product-usage + signup/API-test events → scoring → routing → enrichment → SDR/Sales handoff). The demand **already exists in the system** ("thousands signed up, tested the product, started integrating on their own"); the job is to *instrument, prioritise, and convert* it.

This matters for fit: Mark's day-job reps at [[entities/stackgini]] are **outbound/ABM enterprise motion** ([[skills/ai-powered-sales-tooling]]), structurally closer to fonio. The Nuitée pillars that overlap directly are enrichment + qualification automation (the ABM data layer ports) and AI-as-lever (raw-LLM-API tooling ports). The pillars that are **new reps** are product-usage instrumentation (PostHog event modelling), the SQL/BigQuery product-analytics half, and the PLG instinct of reading product signal as commercial intent. ^[inferred]

## Application angle if pursued

Differentiator vs typical GTM-Engineer applicants (RevOps analysts moving up the stack, growth-ops engineers with direct PLG reps):

1. **Founding-from-zero is the headline, not a footnote.** The JD's repeated "blank page / from scratch / see a gap and fix it / build something from zero" language is the *exact* shape of the Stackgini founding-GTM story. Lead with: built the entire GTM function and its tooling from nothing, sub-$20K → $1M+ ARR. Most applicants have *operated* a GTM machine; Mark has *built one from zero*, which is literally what this seat asks for.
2. **Three shipped AI systems on raw LLM APIs.** [[skills/ai-powered-sales-tooling]] is the artifact this role exists to build. Lead with the ABM data layer (the enrichment + qualification pillar) and the raw-LLM-API automation work (the AI-as-lever pillar). "Use AI to build things faster, not just write emails" is shippable evidence for Mark, not aspiration. Claude-first builder credibility is a direct culture match.
3. **Bridge the PLG gap honestly on transferable systems thinking.** Do not claim PLG reps he lacks ([[feedback_no_self_hedging]]). Frame: the scoring / enrichment / routing / signal-to-noise machine is the same systems problem he has solved for outbound, applied to a different demand source. The ABM data layer *is* a lead-scoring-and-prioritisation system; porting it to product-usage signal is a substitution of inputs, not a new discipline.
4. **AI-native + engineer-seller vocabulary, not demand-gen lexicon.** Mirror the JD's voice: "build the operating system," "track / score / enrich / route / convert," "signal not noise," "from a blank page." Avoid funnel-jargon (MQL/BANT/demand-capture) — wrong register for an engineer-shaped Growth seat.
5. **Why-Nuitée narrative**: API-first infrastructure ("Stripe for Travel") as a category-defining-infra thesis at the moment it stands up PLG as a new motion — i.e. the founding-PLG-infrastructure window is open *now*, which is exactly when an engineer-seller compounds most. Avoid generic "I want to work in travel" — the why is the *founding-PLG-machine-at-a-well-backed-infra-company* opportunity, not the travel domain.

**Counter-signals to weigh before applying:**
- The PLG-motion gap is real and the field has applicants with direct product-led + product-analytics reps. Decide whether to invest screen-prep in articulating the systems-transfer argument convincingly.
- The SQL / PostHog / BigQuery data half is the load-bearing technical screen ("solid with SQL"). If there is a technical case, it likely tests warehouse SQL and product-event modelling — Mark's thinner axis. Worth brushing up before a screen.
- No comp band / equity disclosure — Series-A-stage equity is the asymmetric component; cash alone does not tell you the deal shape.
- Experience floor (3-5 yrs) sits just above Mark's ~2.5 — the founding claim has to carry the gap.
